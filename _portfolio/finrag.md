FinRAG is a financial retrieval-augmented generation application for interrogating SEC filings with natural-language questions. It processes filing documents, retrieves relevant evidence, calculates financial ratios, and generates visualizations to support financial analysis. Built with Python, Streamlit, LangChain, FAISS, and the OpenAI API.
