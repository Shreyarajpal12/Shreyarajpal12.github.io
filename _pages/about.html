--- permalink: / title: "Shreya Rajpal" seo_title: "Shreya Rajpal | Symbolic & Trustworthy AI Researcher" author_profile: true description: "Shreya Rajpal researches symbolic, structured, and trustworthy reasoning across language and vision, neuro-symbolic AI, and spatial reasoning at Michigan State University." redirect_from: - /about/ - /about.html --- I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Science and Engineering at Michigan State University. I am advised by Prof. Parisa Kordjamshidi in the Heterogeneous Learning & Reasoning Lab, where I develop neuro-symbolic methods for symbolic, structured, and trustworthy reasoning across language and vision. I am a University Distinguished Fellow and Dr. Frank B. Martin Cum Rec Endowed Fellow. Previously, I worked on efficient video understanding at the University of Tübingen, trustworthy medical-language-model explanations at IIT Madras, financial LLM applications with Georgia Tech's Financial Services Innovation Lab, and knowledge-graph question answering at the University of Hamburg. In summer 2026, I was an AI Science Intern at Intuit, where I worked on production recommendation systems and recommender migration research. I returned to Michigan State University in August 2026 to continue my Ph.D. My research spans trustworthy AI, spatial reasoning, language models, and vision-language models. My work has appeared at EMNLP, ACM FAccT, ACL workshops, and ISWC workshops.
I am open to collaborations. Let's connect on LinkedIn or email me at rajpalsh@msu.edu.
