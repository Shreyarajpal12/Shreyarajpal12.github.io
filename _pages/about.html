--- permalink: / title: "Shreya Rajpal" author_profile: true description: "Shreya Rajpal is a Ph.D. researcher at Michigan State University studying trustworthy neuro-symbolic AI, spatial reasoning, and language models." redirect_from: - /about/ - /about.html --- I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Science and Engineering at Michigan State University and an AI Science Intern at Intuit. I am advised by Prof. Parisa Kordjamshidi in the Heterogeneous Learning & Reasoning Lab, where I develop trustworthy neuro-symbolic methods for complex spatial reasoning. I am a University Distinguished Fellow and Dr. Frank B. Martin Cum Rec Endowed Fellow. Previously, I worked on efficient video understanding at the University of Tübingen, trustworthy medical-language-model explanations at IIT Madras, financial LLM applications with Georgia Tech's Financial Services Innovation Lab, and knowledge-graph question answering at the University of Hamburg. At Intuit, I work on RAG pipelines and deep-learning product recommendation systems. My work has appeared at ACM FAccT, ACL workshops, and ISWC workshops.
I am open to collaborations. Let's connect on LinkedIn or email me at rajpalsh@msu.edu.
